Calculation notes, provenance and limitations

V9 lowwall: H = D tan(angle). New loose-volume capacity is integrated between the existing pile and the designed inclined surface, clipped by crest and toe bounds. Toe is solved by bisection to fit the requested loose inventory; excess remains unplaced. The design is not an assertion of geotechnical stability. Incomplete actual deposits are not reshaped cosmetically. Cast profile remains a conceptual bulk displacement, not a blast forecast.

Bank inventory = width × strike × OB. Loose material = fragmented bank volume × (1 + swell). Each movement subtracts source bank inventory and adds loose material to a receiving cell. Cast flow fits a conserved source and receiving profile with a shared contact elevation when feasible, subject to capacity and distance. Ten increments illustrate bulk displacement, not physical blast time steps. Bank-volume ledger uses cell inventories; the cross-section is a cell-centre visualization, not an independent volume survey. Intact faces and toe gaps remain explicit. Dozer receiving targets spread fill into a bounded profile; dragline placement is still a cell heuristic without a certified final spoil face. Spoil capacity is a discrete integral of the assumed receiving envelope minus existing surface.

Dozer cycle = 3.6D/vf + 3.6D/vr + other seconds; productivity uses effective blade load, efficiency and swell. Dragline effective loose bucket is limited by reference capacity and assumed payload. Each cut screens approximate digging/dumping radii, depth and dump height. Swing/depth cycle corrections are illustrative.

Based on the prior STRATA Python backend, independently ported to browser JavaScript. TERRA inspires grid and restricted placement; cut/fill allocation uses a deterministic nearest-feasible heuristic, not RL or linear-programming optimisation. No third-party repository source code is bundled. MEC pivot-push and ACARP dozer research inform the workflow. Geometric pivot not solved without a designed/surveyed cross-section. No granular physics, validated blast prediction, track bearing pressure, traction or certified OEM range/load chart verification. Pad readiness screens footprint, stand-off, level and height. V9 uses full-width grade design, highwall-side corridor and staged cut/fill. Receiving targets use a level-fill heuristic with local slope checks. Pivot is a geometric intersection diagnostic, not a globally optimised dozer push design. Local route and slope screens are not stability certification. End recovery is conditional on an independently approved accessible external platform. V9 screens using conservative approximate points below the published 50 m / 40° OEM planning curve (p. 27), additionally capped by user input; bucket payload remains an assumption. Not a certified load/range chart. See 3d-Dig bench design and LMD 1200 reference data. Machine icons and moving arrows are schematic, not simulated rigid-body physics.

Old spoil coverage is the cross-strip footprint percentage, not a volume percentage. Existing spoil uses a one-sided wedge at the input angle and is excluded from new OB accounting. Concept engineering software. Use measured topography, site material properties, qualified mine planning and OEM/geotechnical validation before operational use.
